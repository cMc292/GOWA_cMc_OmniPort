# Trophies: the game's own list, a notice for each one, the platinum (2026-11-18)

Issue #19 asked for trophies. The runtime's `sceNpTrophy` already kept what the game unlocked (a JSON file of ids and
times) and answered the game, but it knew nothing of the set: no names, no grades, 128 trophies, never a platinum, and
nothing on screen. The game itself only calls Init, Create/Destroy Context and Handle, AbortHandle, RegisterContext,
GetRequiredDiskSpace, UnlockTrophy, GetTrophyUnlockState and Term (`ps3recomp/tools/gen_imports.py` on GOWA.elf).

## The set

The game's TROPHY.TRP sits in `TROPDIR/NPWR00802_00` of its update (and of the disc, and of the digital release's
folder). It is a flat archive (`trophy_set.h`): TROPCONF.SFM, TROP.SFM in English, TROP_xx.SFM for 13 more PS3
languages, ICON0.PNG and one 240 x 240 PNG a trophy. 36 trophies: 1 platinum (#0, Champion of the Gods), 5 gold, 10
silver, 20 bronze, 21 of them hidden; every one but the platinum counts toward it (pid 0).

`libs/network/trophy_set.c` reads it: the names from TROP_<system language>.SFM, else TROP.SFM; entities decoded to
UTF-8. Checked on this PC's EU set in English, German, Russian and Japanese. `sceNpTrophyRegisterContext` finds it as
the PS3 does (the folder of PS3_BOOT_PATH's USRDIR, then the disc's; `PS3_TROPHY_TRP` names a file outright) and logs
it: `"God of War: Ascension™", 36 trophies (1 platinum, 5 gold, 10 silver, 20 bronze), 1 unlocked`.

## The platinum

Given as the PS3 gives it: when the last trophy whose pid is the platinum's is unlocked, less those a port marks as not
to be had (`sceNpTrophySetUnobtainable`). Here that is #1 Blood Oath, "Complete the MP Training in Olympus": the
multiplayer is not in the port. It is also checked at RegisterContext, for unlocks kept before this build. A title
unlocking a platinum itself gets PLATINUM_CANNOT_UNLOCK; UnlockTrophy returns the platinum's id when it completes it.

## Where the unlocks are kept

Before, `./gamedata/trophies/<id>.json`, from whatever folder the game was started in; the saves had that problem once
and were moved beside /dev_hdd0. The unlocks now go where the PS3 keeps trophies, beside the saves:
`<hdd0>/home/00000001/trophy/NPWR00802.json` (`$PS3_GAMEDATA_ROOT/trophies` if that is set). The old file is read while
there is none in the new place, and the next unlock writes the new one. Players who carry `gamedata\dev_hdd0\home` to
a new release now carry their trophies too.

## On screen

`rsx_d3d12_backend_trophy` queues a notice: the trophy's picture, "<grade> trophy earned" in the grade's colour and
its name, at the top centre for six seconds, fading in and out; several wait their turn. It is a band of the overlay's
texture of its own (OVT_H, 160 rows under the shader indicator's), drawn with GDI once and faded into the upload
buffer each frame it shows; a message such as "Screenshot saved" moves under it. `port/trophies.cpp` sets the
observer, reads and decodes the picture on a thread of its own (in order), and marks #1.

The launcher has a Trophies tab: the set's name, how much is won and of each grade, then each trophy with its picture
(grey until won), grade and the day it was won. A hidden one shows neither name nor picture until it is won, and #1
says it is not in this port.

## Tested

- Launcher: `GOWA_LAUNCHER_SHOT` with this PC's file (Prison Break, won 17 Nov): 1 of 35, the rows, the end of the list.
- Game, `PS3_GAMEDATA_ROOT` at a scratch folder: with 2-35 kept, the platinum came at RegisterContext and was saved;
  with 3-35 kept, none. F12 screenshots (the swap chain's buffer) showed the bronze, gold and platinum notices and
  "Screenshot saved" under one. `GOWA_TROPHY_DEMO=0,5,35` shows notices without unlocking anything.
- Not tested: a trophy won in play with this build. That path is UnlockTrophy into the same code the
  RegisterContext test went through.
